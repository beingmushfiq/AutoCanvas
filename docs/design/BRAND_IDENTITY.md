# AutoCanvas Brand Identity

## 1. Brand Concept
AutoCanvas represents the seamless convergence of autonomous scheduling and high-craft creative composition.

- **Product Name:** AutoCanvas
- **Domain:** `autocanvas.devcenterpoint.com`
- **Tagline:** One brand. Every channel. Always in rhythm.

## 2. Logo System: "The Frame"
The AutoCanvas symbol is composed of two nested hairline rectangular frames:
- **Outer Frame:** Represents the outer channel envelope (the multi-platform publishing boundary).
- **Inner Frame:** Represents the creative canvas emerging from intelligent composition.
- **Amber Anchor Point:** A solid amber circular accent positioned at the intersection point, symbolizing human intent and creative focus.

### Clearspace & Sizing
- Minimum size: 16px × 16px (Favicon mark).
- Symbol clearspace: 50% of the symbol width on all sides.
- Horizontal lockup: Combines "The Frame" symbol with the "AutoCanvas" wordmark in Geist Sans. In the wordmark, "Auto" is set in SemiBold (600) and "Canvas" in Regular (400).

### Color Variations
1. **Light Background:** Dark charcoal frames (`oklch(14% 0.003 265)`) + Amber dot (`oklch(68% 0.18 60)`).
2. **Dark Background:** Off-white frames (`oklch(96% 0.003 265)`) + Bright Amber dot (`oklch(76% 0.17 65)`).
3. **Monochrome:** Pure black or pure white with no colored dot for laser-engraving or single-color reproduction.
