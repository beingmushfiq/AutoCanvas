## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `cinematic` / `polished`
- Creative direction: "Editorial Studio Precision SaaS Launch Film"
- Interpretation: Deliberate, confident timing; deep obsidian aesthetics; amber glowing interactive highlights; authentic product UI elements; silky GSAP transitions; strict avoidance of cartoony or chaotic gimmicks.
- Angle: Replacing fragmented social media schedulers and messy generative diffusion with an authentic, deterministic multi-tenant operating system.
- Hook: The chaotic fragmentation of multi-channel management collapsing into the unified clarity of AutoCanvas.
- Outro: Official brand logo reveal, punchy headline, verified web domain, and DevCenterPoint attribution.
- Avoid:
  - Generic stock footage or 3D character illustrations
  - Hallucinated or distorted UI screenshots
  - Unreadable text with fast cuts
  - Non-authentic brand colors
